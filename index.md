# Ella IMS

**Ella IMS** lets subscribers of a private 4G or 5G network make voice and video calls. It is an IP Multimedia Subsystem (IMS) that runs as a single application, designed to be easy to operate, reliable, and secure.

## Key features

- **Voice and video calls** over 4G (VoLTE) and 5G (VoNR).
- **SIM-based authentication** with your existing HSS (IMS-AKA over Cx), and IPsec between phones and the IMS.
- **Voice QoS** from the 4G or 5G core: PCRF over Rx, or PCF over N5 with optional mutual TLS.
- **Application Servers**: connect voicemail, conferencing, supplementary services and other services, routed per subscriber by your HSS (iFCs over ISC).
- **Push-to-talk**: a built-in MCPTT server for group calls with floor control, and an Android client.
- **Call records**: who called whom, when, for how long, and how the call ended.
- **Easy to operate**: a single binary with an embedded database, a web UI, an HTTP API, and Prometheus metrics.
